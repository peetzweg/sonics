standing on shoulders

        

          everyone who synthesises sound in the browser aimed at retro game sfx; everyone
          aiming at tasteful ui shipped audio files. plink sits in the empty cell — tiny,
          synth-based, tasteful, with a designer. inspirations:
          zzfx (frank force) for the sub-1 kb
          synth + designer pattern, and
          web-haptics (lochie axon) for the sibling-modality
          design→export loop this mirrors. also
          sfxr,
          jsfxr,
          jfxr,
          snd-lib, and
          use-sound. the default
          click was reverse-engineered from the elevenlabs onboarding sound.
